Dataset acquisition policy — Version 1

Platform:
Sentinel-1A only.

Product:
Sentinel-1 Level-1 IW GRD, dual-polarization VV and VH.

Study area:
Twenty German cities with standardized 30 km × 30 km centred AOIs.

Temporal interval:
10 July 2025 to 20 July 2025 for selected products.

Orbit-direction policy:
Descending acquisitions were preferred.

Exceptions:
Ascending acquisitions were used for Munich (DE05), Berlin (DE14)
and Rostock (DE20), because no single Sentinel-1A descending
acquisition provided at least 99% coverage of the standardized AOI.

Coverage:
All selected products provide at least 99% AOI coverage; selected
products currently provide 100% calculated AOI coverage.

Scene policy:
One product per city. No mosaicking is used in dataset version 1.
